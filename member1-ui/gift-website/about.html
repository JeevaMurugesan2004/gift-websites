About Giftly

Cultivating the lost art of sincere giving through curated craftsmanship, sustainable beauty, and unforgettable unboxing rituals.

Home About Us
Our Genesis

Where Genuine Thought Meets Timeless Design

Founded in 2021, Giftly was born from a simple belief: the most powerful gifts aren't just things—they are cherished emotional milestones.

In an era of rushed online deliveries and disposable wrapping, we felt something sacred was missing from celebrations. The thrill of untying a silk ribbon, the aroma of preserved botanicals, the tactile weight of personalized heavy paperboard—these tactile moments convert a mere item into a lifelong memory.

Today, Giftly collaborates with independent chocolatiers, botanists, master glassblowers, and leather craftsmen to create gifts that stand apart. Every single parcel leaves our Indiranagar studio hand-inspected, wrapped in our signature rose-and-gold presentation, and sealed with a personalized note.

Our Principles

Values That Guide Every Ribbon We Tie

Our standards define everything from the sustainable sourcing of our rigid gift boxes to the precision of our calligraphers.

Artisanal Sourcing

We reject mass-produced generic goods in favor of limited-batch delicacies, real preserved florals, and master handcrafted keepsakes.

Eco-Luxury Standard

100% biodegradable ribbons, FSC-certified rigid boxes, and zero single-use plastics without ever compromising showroom glamour.

Bespoke Detail

From hot-foil gold embossing to custom constellation night skies, our customization options make every present one of a kind.

White-Glove Care

Climate-controlled logistics ensure your artisanal truffles never melt and eternal Parisian roses arrive petal-perfect.

Empathy First

We listen closely. If an anniversary or emergency birthday gift needs urgent courier rerouting, our team treats it as our own.

Delight Guarantee

If the recipient isn't completely enchanted by their Giftly unboxing experience, we will replace or refund it without friction.

Our Journey

Milestones of Meaning

From our humble beginnings in a small Bangalore atelier to delivering across 50+ major cities.

The First Ribbon Tied

Giftly began as a bespoke bridal atelier crafting custom memory boxes for intimate ceremonies.

2022

Indiranagar Flagship Studio

Opened our flagship floral and hampers curation showroom, welcoming over 2,000 visitors in our debut year.

2023

2024
